-------------------------------------------------------------------------

  compiler -- no JDK/Kotlin toolchain is available in this sandbox.
- That it runs correctly on the JVM or on a real Android device/emulator
  -- no `adb`, no Android SDK, no emulator image available (confirmed:
  `which gradle`/`which adb` both fail, `$ANDROID_HOME` unset).
- Anything about the `android/` placeholder shell (Task 203) actually
  building with Gradle -- see `android/README.md`'s own "not
  build-validated" section.

This is real evidence of a real, working binding-generation pipeline
(Scaffolded and Implemented-and-tested at the Rust/codegen layer), not a
claim of a validated end-to-end Kotlin/Android build (which remains
Implemented-but-not-hardware-validated / Scaffolded, honestly, until a
real Android toolchain is available to verify it).
